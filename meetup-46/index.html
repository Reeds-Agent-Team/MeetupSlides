Bitcoin History · The Meme That's Actually True
NGU Technology

"Number Go Up" — the half-joking name for Bitcoin's tendency to rise in price over time.

Supply is capped at 21 million forever. As more people save in it, the same fixed pie gets divided among more demand — so the number goes up.

It's a meme, sure. But it's still fundamentally true — it just doesn't feel true while the price is dipping.
